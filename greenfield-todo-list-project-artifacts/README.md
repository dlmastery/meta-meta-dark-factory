# Greenfield Todo And Habits Benchmark Artifacts

Status: PRD clarification v0.4 applied, still awaiting customer approval or edits to remaining detailed choices.

This package is the first factory step for a greenfield todo-and-habits reference benchmark. The app is a simulation workload for testing the dark-factory meta-skills, not the ultimate product the factory exists to build. It deliberately starts with customer interrogation and validation before deeper design or implementation.

Artifacts:

- `00-customer-interrogation.md`: questions to develop and validate the spec.
- `01-project-profile-draft.md`: initial product profile and assumptions.
- `02-requirements-draft.md`: draft functional and non-functional requirements.
- `03-acceptance-scenarios.md`: scenario seeds for validation.
- `04-artifact-plan-and-traceability.md`: planned artifact set and traceability skeleton.
- `05-product-requirements-document-40-page.md`: controlling 40-page PRD draft with customer interrogation and validation gates.
- `06-prd-triple-critic-review.md`: three-critic review against the original dark-factory requirements.
- `07-prd-finding-fix-report.md`: record of fixes applied to the critic findings.
- `08-prd-benchmark-anti-overfit-revision.md`: revision record for treating todo/habits as a benchmark app, not the ultimate target.
- `09-meta-skill-self-application-check.md`: self-application check showing how the meta-skill process was applied to this PRD revision.
- `10-prd-clarification-1234-revision.md`: revision record applying the customer `1,2,3,4` clarification across benchmark intent, habit scope, excellence dimensions, and backend/sync/production stress paths.

Gate: these artifacts are not final until the customer approves or edits the captured `1,2,3,4` interpretation and the remaining PRD details.
