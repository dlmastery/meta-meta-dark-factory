# Research Watch And Source Freshness Protocol

## Purpose

The original transcript repeatedly asked for latest dark-factory, multi-agent, SDLC, GitHub, and arXiv context. DFMS must not freeze a 2026 answer forever. It needs a governed source-refresh loop.

## Freshness Rule

Any DFMS spec that claims "latest", "state of the art", "current", "frontier", "best open source", "as of 2026", model availability, standards currency, or tool capabilities must carry:

- source date;
- source URL;
- source type: primary, official, paper, vendor, community, benchmark, or anecdote;
- confidence;
- last checked date;
- recheck cadence;
- stale-after date;
- impact if source changes.

If a source is stale, the factory may continue only by:

1. marking the claim stale and non-binding;
2. refreshing from primary sources;
3. recording a waiver with owner and residual risk.

## Research Watch RALPH Gate

Every research refresh must run:

1. Review: identify changed sources and claims.
2. Attack: challenge whether sources are primary, current, and relevant.
3. Learn: extract only durable patterns, separating inspiration from binding requirements.
4. Patch: update inspiration maps, standards baseline, role/rubric library, or workflow gates.
5. Harden: record source freshness, changed claims, and unresolved uncertainty.

## Anti-Slop Rules

- Do not cite a product feature unless the source actually supports it.
- Do not treat search snippets as full proof when the page can be opened.
- Do not encode a vendor-specific feature as a DFMS requirement unless it improves the transcript vision.
- Do not imply external certification from internal DFMS certificates.
- Do not use anecdotal community posts as primary evidence for architecture claims.

Each governed research refresh must produce `research-source-freshness-record.json` with:

- query set;

- stale claims;
- next recheck date;

- RALPH loop result.
